Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: finished square project icon for StillHere, a volunteer journey companionship app.
Primary request: Create ONE exceptional, polished minimalist brand symbol expressing continuous companionship: a human guardian stays with you, and when they rest, their personal agent continues the authorized presence.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly uniform near-black #0c0e11 square background.
Subject: a single bold, elegantly proportioned emblem made from two continuing rounded companion arcs, intertwined into an abstract S. Style/medium: precise flat vector-like graphic logo with exceptionally clean edges, solid fills, optical balance, professional contemporary brand identity. No texture, shading, glow or 3D.
Composition/framing: 1024x1024 square image, centered symbol occupying approximately 62% of canvas width and height, ample equal safe margins, strong recognizability at 32 pixels.
Text: absolutely no text, letters, wordmark, numerals, caption, signature or watermark; the S is only suggested abstractly through the forms.
Constraints: One finished icon only, no presentation board, no grid, no mockup. Bold simple silhouette, at most two continuous colored shapes. Avoid crypto/coin/currency imagery, literal dollar signs, medical crosses, police/security badges, locks, eyes, circuit traces, literal robots, hand illustrations, hearts, gradients, glossy lighting, tiny details.
